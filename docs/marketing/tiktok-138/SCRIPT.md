## Why this one

A grievance every couple has (left on delivered), a number (4 hours, at 5pm) and an absurd escalation: court. The open loop is *what court*, and slide 2 answers it with five cats behind a desk, which is the swipe. He does the selling without knowing it: every feature arrives as him being confused by it — "why does one have a shark hat", "the cat with glasses just read out…", "HOW DOES IT KNOW THAT", "it stamped GUILTY on a certificate with my name on". The findings are the inside-joke engine: viewers immediately write their own in their heads, which is the purchase. The tag is built in ("tag someone who needs to be taken to court"), and the last line hands it to the other half of the audience: the boys want to sue back.

## The slides

1. The stop. Hook over the thread: "4 hours" / "i was asleep" / "at 5pm" / "its called a nap". Petty, specific, no explanation.
