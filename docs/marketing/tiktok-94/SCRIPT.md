## Why it works in four

- **The hook is a date.** "he made it 3 days after we met" is a fact, not a feeling, and a fact is
  what stops a scroll. There is nothing to interpret and nothing to argue with.
- **"dont look at the date" is the best first bubble we have written.** It tells the viewer where to
  look while pretending to do the opposite, and it costs four words.
- **The number does everything.** Three days. Fourteen months. He has been carrying it around for
  over a year, and the reason he finally sent it is that she said something offhand about how long he
  took to fall for her. He did not argue. He produced evidence.
- **Nothing is confessed out loud.** "thats the thing" is as close as he gets to saying it.
