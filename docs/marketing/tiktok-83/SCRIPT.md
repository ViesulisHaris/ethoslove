Post **21:00 UK**, couple account or the personal one. Sales shape: four chat slides, six gift frames.

## Why this one is built to sell

- **The viewer is the one who made it.** Every blue bubble is hers. In 80, 81 and 82 the narrator
  receives the gift, and the feeling that leaves is "I wish someone would". Here the feeling is "I
  could do that and they would lose it", which is the one that opens a link. Put the buyer in the
  maker's seat whenever the story allows it.
- **The premise is the reason it is personal.** Every friendship has a folder of photos one of them
  has asked the other to delete. "i kept everything" is the whole gift in three words, and it tells
  the viewer exactly which photos of their own to go and find.
- **"theres tape on them" / "theyre pages"** is the product description, said as a reaction. It is the
  difference between this and a camera roll, and it is why somebody pays for it.
- **The last line is the promise:** "im deleting all of these btw" / "u cant. theyre pages now". A made
  thing, not a folder. It closes the joke and states what the money buys, in seven words.
- **"half an hour, and the photos took longer than the making"** kills the only real objection. It is
  also true, which matters when somebody tries it.
- **Midnight is the friendship detail.** Waiting up to send it at 00:01 is what people do for the
  people they love, and it costs nothing to copy.
- **Nobody is a bad anything.** There is no partner in this story at all, which widens it: everybody
  has a best friend's birthday coming, and most people have several a year. That is a far more
  frequent buying moment than an anniversary.

Her: the one who kept them. Mimi: the one who asked her not to. Four chat slides, six gift frames.

## To make it a partner's birthday instead

Change two bubbles and nothing else: "ITS 12AM" → "its 12am why are you awake", and "i asked u to
delete those" → "i asked you to delete that one". Swap year 7 and year 9 for the first month and last
summer. Everything else works as written.
