## Why this one

The tactic is **isolation**: the breakup threat as a lever, the friends as the crime, the gift as the reward for choosing him. Every line of his is one people will name in the comments:

- "u went out. i asked u not to": the rule she did not know she had agreed to.
- "i made u this before u decided ur friends matter more": the gift as a debt.
- "i never said u couldnt go": the rule, denied a slide later.
- "i just need to know im ur priority": the ask underneath.
- "ur choice. u always have a choice maya": the threat, phrased as freedom. The last words on screen.

And **she takes the blame**: "i wont go next friday. i'll tell jess." The caption is her asking whether that was right. The comments answer.
