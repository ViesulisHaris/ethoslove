## Why this one

The tactic is **the gift instead of the apology**, and then turning it round:

- "u embarrassed me" / "in front of my friends": what he did becomes what she did.
- "i dont want to fight. i made u this instead": the present in the place where "sorry" goes.
- "lets not tell ur sister about last night": the ask to keep it quiet, dressed as peace.
- "i made u a whole thing maya. what more do u want": the gift as a bill.
- "see. we're good" / "delete this thread": the last lines, and the reason this is a screenshot.

She says the true thing once ("i just wish u could say sorry") and takes it back a bubble later. That reversal is what people will quote.
