## Why it works

- **"do u even like me" at 2am is the most real message in any of these.** Everybody at that age has
  sent it and regretted it within ten seconds, which is what the next two bubbles are: "sorry ignore
  that" / "im tired".
- **He does not reassure her, he goes and gets something.** "give me 5 mins" is worth more than any
  paragraph, and it is what somebody who cannot talk about feelings actually does.
- **"idk it felt weird" is the confession** and it is the least eloquent sentence possible. He made it
  in June, it is September, and the reason he never sent it is that he was embarrassed.
- **"WEIRD" in caps, on its own, ends it.** No conclusion, no lesson, no "i love you" — she is just
  shouting one word at 3am. That is where a real conversation stops.
