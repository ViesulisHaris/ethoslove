## Why this one is built to sell

- **Four chat slides, six gift frames.** Every earlier carousel is the other way round. The buyer has
  to want the thing, so the thing gets six slides and the story gets four.
- **The dialogue is a demo.** "Is that our song", "blow the candles out", "20 minutes" — every line
  names something the product does. A viewer who has never seen it knows by slide 3 that it plays
  their song and listens to their breath.
- **"20 minutes on the bus" is the whole post.** The objection is never "I don't want to", it is "I
  haven't got time and I'm not creative". Twenty minutes on a bus kills both. Never say four days in a
  sales post; that was right for 77 and wrong here.
- **Blowing out the candles is the screenshot people send each other.** It is the one thing nobody
  believes until they try it, and it is worth a whole frame of the recording.
- **Premium template, on purpose.** From our own sales data: about six in ten people who start a
  Birthday Cinema pay for it, against about one in twenty on the free Letter. People make the template
  they saw. Show the one that earns.
- **The birthday is named in the caption** so everyone with one coming up puts themselves in it.
