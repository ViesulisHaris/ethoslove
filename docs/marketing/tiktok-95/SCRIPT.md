## Why the hook is a row about a phone

A headline written over a picture reads as an advert. A first bubble that is a real complaint reads as
somebody's actual life, and this is the most common complaint there is: you have been on your phone
all week. Every girl watching has sent that message. She is also slightly in the wrong, which is the
part that makes people send it to their boyfriend — it is self-deprecating, so nobody is being told
off, and there is no "bad bf" comment to leave.

- **"ok" is the whole of slide 1's tension.** One word, deflecting, and it makes her angrier. That is
  how it actually goes.
- **"give me a sec" is what he types instead of defending himself.** He is not being mysterious, he is
  buying four minutes to finish the thing.
- **The peak is not a line, it is a mess.** "ur so annoying" / "im crying" is what somebody types when
  they feel too much at once. Nobody lands a sentence at that moment, and writing one is what makes a
  script read as written.
- **"the 2nd" does the confessing.** He has been at it for three weeks and says it in two words.
