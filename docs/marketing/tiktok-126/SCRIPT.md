## Why this one

Impossible-sounding claim (curiosity gap: how do you blow out candles from 4,000 miles?), resolved by the product. Long-distance is the highest-intent buyer segment there is: they can't be there, and the gift is a link.

## The slides

1. The stop. "what time is it there" / "7. why" sets the distance in two lines, "ur birthday starts in 1 min for me" sets the clock.
2. The turn. 12:00 AM stamp, link card, "i couldnt post a card in time. so i made one" — the confession that makes the product the hero. "WAIT IT OPENED".
3. Proof. The lit card standing open: the letter on the lid in handwriting, the paper cake, five flames.
4. She sells it. "it says make a wish" / "marco im on the subway" / "a man looked at me" / "the candles actually went out". The microphone moment told as an embarrassing story, which is how a feature becomes a memory.
5. Proof two. The letter, open.
6. "i can see u writing it" / "i miss u so much it hurts" / "31 days" / "31 days" ❤️. A countdown as the last line is the quotable, and the most-repeated comment format ("how many days for you?").
7. The save. "for the long distance ppl asking": template, mic, 20 min, code LOVE, "send it at midnight. theirs or urs, doesnt matter".
