## Why this one is the one to copy

- **She made it.** 85, 86 and 87 put the viewer in the seat of the person receiving; this one puts her
  in the seat of the person who made it, and that is the seat that buys. The feeling it leaves is "I
  could do that and he would be sat in a car about it".
- **"ellie"** — one word, his only serious bubble in the whole first slide. That is how a real person
  uses somebody's name, and it does the same work as a novel's "The end, Mara." without sounding
  written.
- **"im sat in the car outside my own house" / "because i cant go in yet"** is the best line in this
  batch. He is not saying anything about how he feels; he is telling her where he is sitting, and the
  viewer does the rest. Show, do not say, done in a text.
- **"im coming over" twice.** The repeat is the whole answer to "its 11pm". Real people repeat
  themselves when they have made up their mind, and the doubled line is what gets screenshotted.
- **"u kept the receipt from the first night" / "i keep everything"** tells the viewer exactly what to
  go and find in their own drawer, which is what makes somebody open the link.
- **Two weeks is honest and still achievable.** She collected for two weeks and made it in an evening.
  Nobody is intimidated by two weeks of keeping receipts.

Her: the one who nearly didn't send it. Him: Sam, in a car. No villain anywhere.
