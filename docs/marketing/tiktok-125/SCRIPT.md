## Why this one

Confession + a number. "Forgot" is a mistake everyone has made (identity trigger), "11:41pm" is specific enough to be true (specificity), and the promise is a rescue with 19 minutes on the clock (stakes). Friendship, not romance, so the share is "tag the friend", which is the widest tag there is.

## The slides

1. The stop. Hook over four bubbles: a friend being told not to sleep at 11:41. Nothing is explained. The swipe is the question.
2. The turn. "it took me 17 minutes" is the confession inside the confession, then the link card with her name on it. "why are there balloons in my phone" ends the slide mid-reaction.
3. Proof. The room: the 30, her name, five balloons popped and the polaroids on the floor. The pattern interrupt from grey screenshots to colour is what earns the dwell.
4. She sells it. "THEY HAVE PHOTOS IN THEM" / "u kept that photo??" / "u did this in 17 minutes" — every feature in her words, then "on the sofa. dont tell anyone", which is the buyer's permission to do it quickly.
5. Proof two. The letter with the polaroid garland: the depth behind the trick.
6. The quotable line and the heart: "no im reading it again". Then "my sister wants one. how" — someone in the story wants one before the viewer does.
7. The save. A Notes screenshot answering the comments: template, how, 17 min, code LOVE, "send it at 12:00 exactly". Saves are the heaviest signal after the swipe-through; this slide is what people save.
