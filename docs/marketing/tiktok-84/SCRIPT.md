## First: 100 views is not a script problem

A post that did 20k and then one that did 100 from the same account is not a writing problem, it is a
distribution one. A script cannot move a post 200x. What does:

- **Too many posts too close together.** A young account gets one cold-start pool at a time. Two or
  three posts in a day splits it and every one of them dies. One post a day, per account.
- **The same picture every time.** Black background, white bubbles, same layout, posted again and
  again — that is what an unoriginal-content filter is for. Alternate: iMessage one day, Instagram DMs
  (`"style": "instagram"` in the script) the next, and start some carousels on a gift frame instead
  of a chat so the thumbnail is not another black rectangle.
- **A first slide that says nothing.** Slide 1 is the thumbnail and the two-second test at once. "You
  up?" on black gives a stranger no reason to stay. Every carousel from here gets a hook drawn into
  slide 1 — the renderer does it now, `"hook": "..."` on the slide.
- **A domain in the caption.** Keep tryethos.io out of the caption and the on-screen text. It goes in
  the pinned comment, where it does not cost reach.
- **No first hour.** Reply to every comment for the first sixty minutes. On photo posts that is most
  of the ranking signal you can still influence after posting.

Post this one on a day when nothing else goes out from that account, and if it still does 100, the
account is the problem and not the post — start a second one and post the same carousel there.

## Why this one should travel further than 83

- **The whole carousel is a conversation with his mum,** which we have never done. New picture, new
  voice, no partner in it. That alone breaks the visual and tonal sameness that gets posts filtered.
- **"I have 400" is the hook doing its job in the sixth bubble.** It is funny, it is a number, and it
  tells the viewer this is going somewhere. A stranger will swipe for that.
- **It hands over an idea, not a feeling.** "Text their mum for baby photos" is a thing a viewer can
  do tonight without buying anything first — and the only place to put 38 baby photos is in something
  like this. That is the softest possible sell and the highest-intent one.
- **The mum is the proof.** She has not seen the seaside photo in twenty years, she is in bits, he has
  rung her twice. Somebody who is not in the couple and not selling anything says the gift is good.
- **"half an hour. u did the hard part"** kills the effort objection and is a compliment at the same
  time, which is exactly how you talk to somebody's mum.
- **Nobody is upset anywhere in it.** The comments have nothing to argue about, so they go to "im
  texting his mum right now", which is the comment that turns into a sale.

Him: Theo, 20. Her: the one who asked. Liz: capital letters, full sentences, "Love Liz x".
