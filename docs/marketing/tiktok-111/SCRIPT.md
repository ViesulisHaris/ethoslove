# TikTok carousel 111 · "he's drunk"

Three slides, dark iMessage, US timestamps — built on a drunk-text post the user sent in (slide 1
kept word for word). Post **23:00 UK** for the US evening.

## What the original does

- **Slide 1 is the hook, with no hook text.** 5:10 AM, a curt "What do u want", and somebody else
  answering from his phone: "ignore it" / "he’s drunk". Three questions in three bubbles — who
  texted her, what did he want, why is his friend holding his phone.
- **The slides are one thread, scrolling.** Slide 2 is slide 1 plus what came next. Nothing is
  re-explained, and every swipe feels like reading somebody's actual phone.
- **Typos are the proof it's real.** Theirs is "gooodnpeeson". Only the drunk messages have them;
  her phone autocorrects ("What do u want").
- **A heart on the line that hurts.** Theirs sits on "you’ll find someone better".

## Ours

At 6:13 the drunk message is the thing he made sober in June and never sent. In the morning he
blames the drink, and her answer is the date.

**Why it sells.** The gift is the whole plot. The link title says what is inside it, so the gift is
the confession he couldn't make, and "U weren’t drunk in June" turns making one into saying it
sober. The buyer is every viewer with something they can't say out loud. Nobody is a villain: the
friend is covering for him, and he is embarrassed, not cruel.
