## Saved replies (Etsy calls them snippets)

Fast answers count towards the shop's service score and, after 5 orders, the Star Seller badge.
Never promise anything the site doesn't do.

All five are in Etsy since 28 Oct 2026. Use them from the snippet icon under any message box.
"Save as a new snippet" there saves whatever is in the box. Etsy's Manage snippets list shows
apostrophes as `&#39;`, but the inserted text is correct.

**Where's my gift? (EN)**
Hi! Your gift is made on our site, nothing to download: go to tryethos.io/etsy and type your order number (it's in Etsy's email, after "Order #"). Your gift opens on your phone: add their name, your photos and your words, then press Send, and its link is ready to share. If anything doesn't work, reply here and I'll sort it out.

**¿Dónde está mi regalo? (ES)**
¡Hola! Tu regalo se crea en nuestra web, no hay que descargar nada: entra en tryethos.io/es/etsy y escribe tu número de pedido (está en el correo de Etsy, después de «Order #»). Tu regalo se abre en tu móvil: añade su nombre, tus fotos y tus palabras, pulsa Enviar y el enlace está listo para compartir. Si algo no funciona, contéstame aquí y lo arreglo.

**"Order not found" or "payment not confirmed" (EN)**
Etsy can take a few minutes to confirm a new payment. Please try again in 5 to 10 minutes at tryethos.io/etsy. If it still says that, send me a screenshot and I'll fix it right away.

**It doesn't open on their phone (EN)**
I'm sorry! Tell me which phone and browser they used and I'll check it today. If we can't make it work, I'll refund your order in full.

**Thank you (EN, after a gift is sent)**
Thank you for your order! I hope they loved it. If you ever need another one, every gift in the shop is made the same way.
