A cloud session can't work on Etsy: etsy.com answers server requests with a captcha (checked 28 Oct, HTTP 403).
So Etsy work needs this PC, or the owner's phone.

## Runs by itself, PC on or off

- **Buyers get their gift** at tryethos.io/etsy (Vercel).
- **The daily Etsy job** (05:00 UTC) takes back refunded orders and keeps the shop connected.
- **Sales:** LAUNCH40 (−40%) ends 22 Nov on its own; Cyber Specials (−40%) runs 23 Nov – 1 Dec, already set up in Etsy.
- **Pinterest** posts the 15 scheduled pins by 2 Nov, then the 29 wave-3 pins from 3 to 11 Nov. Pinterest schedules at most 14 days ahead, so nothing is planned after 11 Nov.
- **Offsite Ads** stay on: Etsy pays, and takes 15% only of a sale they bring.

## Only with the PC on and the Claude app open

- **Etsy rank checks:** the 29 Oct 10:00 one-off never fired (the PC was asleep), so it was run by hand at 12:55. All 54 listings are indexed now: both waves show in search sorted by Newest. In the default (relevance) sort, none is in the top ~120 for any of the 12 test searches, not even by exact title. That is normal for listings a day or two old with no views, favourites or sales. Pinterest clicks, the 40% sale and, from about 12 Nov, Etsy Ads are what move them up.
- **The other desktop tasks:** HALDEN inbox/tracking and the VID PVN on the 10th.

## From the phone

1. **Messages within 24 hours.** Use the "Sell on Etsy" app. The five saved replies (Etsy calls them snippets) are under the snippet icon below the message box: where's my gift (EN/ES), order not found, doesn't open on their phone, thank you.
2. **Etsy Ads from about 12 Nov.** Etsy emails when the 15-day wait for new shops ends. Steps:
   - In the app, go to Marketing → Etsy Ads (or open etsy.com/your/shops/me/advertising in the phone's browser).
   - Set the daily budget to $5 (approved: $3–5).
   - Promote all listings, then start.
   - After 14 days, stop any listing with 30+ clicks and no sale.
3. **Refunds** are the owner's to give, in the app under the order → Refund. The daily job then takes the gift offline.
