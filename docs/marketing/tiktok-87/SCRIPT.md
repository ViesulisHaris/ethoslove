## Why it works, and what it sells

- **"give me a sec im nervous"** is the realistic version of intensity. A boy who is shy about a huge
  feeling reads as true; a boy who is commanding about it reads as written.
- **"u say something at 0:48"** is the best comment engine in this batch. It is a specific timestamp
  inside a thing nobody can hear, so the comments fill with "WHAT DOES HE SAY AT 0:48" — and he
  refuses to tell her, which means nobody can settle it.
- **It sells the extra, which is where the money is on a free template.** A Letter is free; a Letter
  with a voice note in it is not. This whole carousel is twenty seconds of his own voice, whispered,
  recorded next to her while she was asleep, and the frames make the waveform the hero.
- **The eleven months is the confession.** He had it the whole time and said nothing because he did
  not think she felt the same. "so was i. thats why i recorded it" answers why anyone records anything
  at 3am.
- **Nobody is at fault.** She said it first in March, he felt it in November, they were both scared.
  No villain, no argument, nothing to take sides about.
