## Why it works

- **"so ur not gonna say anything" is a real opening message,** and "its fine" is the most loaded two
  words in the language. Everybody watching knows exactly what is happening and exactly how wrong she
  is about to be.
- **The timestamp does the reveal.** Slide 1 is 21.40 and slide 2 is 00.00. He was never going to
  forget, he was waiting for midnight, and the clock says so without anybody explaining it.
- **"about" and "??" are him being genuinely confused,** not playing dumb. That is the bit that makes
  it land rather than annoy.
- **"i hate u" / "ya" is the ending.** Nobody types "i love you" at the end of a real conversation like
  this. They type the opposite and both of them know what it means. Write the opposite.
- **Nobody is a bad boyfriend and nobody is a nag.** She was annoyed, she was wrong, she says so. The
  comments go to "i would have started a whole argument", not to an argument.
