## Why this one is built to sell

- **Long distance is the highest-intent audience on the app.** They cannot post a present, cannot
  turn up, cannot hand over flowers. A digital gift is not a substitute for them, it is the only
  option, and "I cant send you flowers from here" says that in the first slide without pitching
  anything.
- **The story is the demo.** Every line she types is her scrolling: it keeps going, there is a meadow,
  the photos are on the trellis, there is a greenhouse at the end. A viewer who has never heard of us
  now knows exactly what the thing does, described by somebody who is not selling it.
- **"Trellis" / "the TRELLIS" is the joke that keeps it human** and stops four slides of feature
  description reading as an advert.
- **The last line is the pitch and it is hers:** "im 3000 miles away and im in a garden". Nobody
  argues with that, they screenshot it.
- **Newest template, biggest novelty.** Nobody scrolling has seen a gift that grows as you scroll, and
  novelty is what makes somebody stop and open a link instead of scrolling on.

Her: Liv, three time zones away. Him: the one who cannot post flowers. Four chat slides, six gift
frames — and the six are the product, so shoot the scroll properly.
