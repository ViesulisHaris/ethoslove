## Why four

Everything from 77 to 91 is nine or ten slides, and that was the wrong instinct. Swipes are not the
signal — **finishing** is. A ten-slide carousel that loses most people by slide three reads to the
algorithm as a post nobody wanted; a four-slide one that almost everybody finishes reads as a post
that worked. Four slides also means the hook is a third of the post, not a tenth of it.

What survived the cut: the shocking line, the link, one frame of the thing, the payoff. What went:
the setup, the banter, the gift tour. If a bubble is not doing one of those four jobs it is gone.

- **The hook accuses him of something.** "i found a link in his phone with my name on it" reads as a
  cheating story for about two seconds, which is exactly long enough. The relief is the post.
- **"its not finished" / "now its finished"** is the whole arc in two bubbles, forty minutes apart.
- **"i was waiting for u to say it first"** is the line people screenshot, and it makes him the one
  who has been holding something back, not her.
- **She says it first anyway.** Nobody is a bad partner, nobody is owed an apology, so the comments go
  to "what was in it" rather than to an argument.
