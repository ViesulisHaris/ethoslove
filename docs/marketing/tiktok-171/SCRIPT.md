## Why this one

The tactic is **the silent treatment, with a soft block on top of it**:

- **The soft block is the hook.** Everybody under 25 knows exactly what restricted means and what it is for, and it is visible proof of the silence.
- **Three days, and a gym story on day 2.** He wasn't unreachable, he was unreachable to her.
- **"i needed to think without u watching me"**: the silence becomes her surveillance.
- **Her crime is confessed in the middle**, not at the end, so he can use it: she rang his mate and made a fake account.
- **"delete the other account. i mean it"** and her "its gone", Read at 23:58. She complies, he says nothing, the thread ends.
