The number is the whole post. 23 is small enough to be true and large enough to be a confession, and
it is evidence of feeling rather than of surveillance — he can see that it was opened, not where she
was, and everyone who has ever sent something and refreshed the page knows that feeling from the other
side. Her denial is the comedy ("it does not say that"), her "some of them were accidents" is the
shame that makes it love instead of a flex, and he hands the number back to her in the last bubble,
which is the line the comments repeat. It also teaches the one thing about the product that makes
people send one: you can tell when they opened it.
