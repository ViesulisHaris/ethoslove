## Why this one

"No fuss" is what every boyfriend says and nobody believes, so the hook is a rule being broken on a technicality: it isn't a party, it's ten animals in a phone. The number does the work (10 animals, 6 photos, "thats nothing"). He sells it by reporting what the animals said to him — the puppies snitching ("u cried making this") is the detail people quote, and it tells the buyer the best feature without naming it: you write what the animals say.
