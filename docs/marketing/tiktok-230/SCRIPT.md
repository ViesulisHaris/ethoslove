to five bubbles each, and one line at the end that changes the whole chat.

are only the chat, and anything that looks like an ad reads as made.

## How it's made

`"crop": true` in script.json: each slide is 1206px wide and only as tall as the messages, like a real
screenshot. For the most real version of all, type the chat between two phones and screenshot it: the
slides are the script.
