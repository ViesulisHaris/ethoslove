Sending the wrong thing to the wrong chat is a universal fear, and the wrong chat being his mum turns
dread into comedy in one bubble. Mum texts like a mum — capital letters, full sentences, "Love Mum x"
on every message, no mercy — and that contrast carries the whole carousel. The second half turns
warm without anyone saying anything soft: his mum read it twice, his dad cried, and the girlfriend has
not even seen it yet. It is also the rare one that shows the gift being read by somebody who is not in
love with the sender, which is the strongest proof it is any good.
