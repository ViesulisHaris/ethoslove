## Why this one

"Went quiet for 4 minutes" is a result-first hook with a number: the reaction is stated before the gift is shown, so the viewer swipes to see what caused it. The maker is a woman, the recipient a man who cries: it is the version the female buyer (most of them) sees herself in.

## The slides

1. The stop. "dont open this at work" is a dare; the link card carries his name; "opening it" is the last line, so the swipe is the opening.
2. The turn. "u drew a cake" / "wait its drawing itself" / "turn the page", then a timestamp jump 9:13 → 9:17 with nothing in it. That gap is the 4 minutes. "sorry. i had to put my phone down for a sec".
3. Proof. The photos page: taped photos with handwritten captions — "the first coffee. you took my sugar."
4. He sells it. "the captions r in ur handwriting" / "i didnt think ud remember the sugar thing" / "i remember everything. thats the problem" — the line people quote.
5. Proof two. The letter page, in handwriting, addressed to him.
6. "a grown man. in a cubicle" / "happy birthday sam" ❤️ / "my mate just asked what site this is" — the social proof is a stranger in the story wanting the link.
7. The save. "since 200 of u asked": template, how the captions work, "the handwriting is a font, im not talented" (removes the objection), 25 min, code LOVE.
