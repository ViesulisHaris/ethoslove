## Why this one

A risk taken in public: he labelled cat memes with her habits and sent it to her at work. The open loop is whether she's furious, and "its not an apology i havent done anything" / "thats exactly what someone whos done something would say" is the exchange people screenshot. The product's whole joke is in her caps: WHY IS IT LABELLED 'MIA BEFORE COFFEE'. Every viewer instantly writes their own labels, which is the purchase. Dread turns to "im printing the shark one for my desk".
