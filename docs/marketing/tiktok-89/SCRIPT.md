## Why this one is shaped for reach and not for the sale

Our own playbook has said from the start that "Part 2" is the most reliable comment on the app, and we
have never once built one. This is that post.

- **The product is withheld on purpose.** Part 1 shows a link nobody opens. There is nothing to
  screenshot, nothing to explain, nothing to buy — which is exactly why people ask. Part 2 then
  inherits every commenter, and part 2 is where the six gift frames live.
- **The only question in the caption is one word: "should i post what was in it".** No debate about a
  bad boyfriend, no "is this too much" — just a request the viewer can grant in two taps. Comments
  saying "PART 2" are the cheapest engagement on TikTok and they push a post further than an argument.
- **"i know. i can see" / "it tells me when u open it"** is the tension device and a product feature at
  the same time. He can see she has not opened it, which is why she cannot quietly cheat, and every
  viewer who has ever sent anything wants to know that feature exists.
- **Nobody is upset.** He is not angry that she opened it, she is not in trouble. It is forty minutes
  of a girl holding a phone like a bomb, which is funny and stressful and nothing to argue about.
- **It costs nothing to make.** Five chat slides, no screen recording, no gift to build first. Post it
  while the scrapbook you are shooting is still half done.
