## Why this one is built to sell

- **It answers the only objection that matters: "is a digital gift a cop-out?"** She asks the price
  and he answers with a comparison, not a number. Then she says the line herself — "and they dont die"
  — and he repeats it. The product promise, said twice, by the person receiving it. That is the whole
  post and it takes four bubbles.
- **"Check ur messages" is the hook and the tutorial.** In one bubble the viewer learns that flowers
  can arrive in a text, which is the idea they are going to copy.
- **Every line is a feature.** You pick each stem from a list, your favourite is in there because they
  were listening, and there is a card tucked in the paper. Three features, no adjectives.
- **Nobody is upset.** He did not forget anything, she is not testing him. There is nothing to
  argue about, so the comments go to "what is this" and "sending this to mine", which are the two that
  convert.
- **Premium, and the second-best converter we have.** About six in ten people who start a Bouquet pay
  for it.
- **"For no reason" is the biggest occasion there is.** Anniversaries come once; a Tuesday comes
  every week. The caption says it outright so the viewer does not need a date to act.
