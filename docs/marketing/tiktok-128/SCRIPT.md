## Why this one

A mistake in progress (dread → comedy) and a group, so the viewer's own group chat is the tag. Four people react on screen, which is four testimonials that don't look like testimonials, and the reveal ("ana is in this chat") sends people back a slide to check — reverse swipes count.

## The slides

1. The stop. Blue asks the group to review it before it's sent; the link card with her name; two friends react. The names over the grey bubbles make it a group without saying so.
2. The turn. Priya: candles. Tom: "i just blew on my phone in a lidl". Jess: "the film is our photos??" — three features, three voices. "its from all of us. i put everyone in".
3. Proof. The cake with the candles lit, the curtain, the ticket with her name.
4. The pivot. "20 mins. u just upload the photos" (the how, in passing), then Jess: "wait" / "guys" / "ana is in this chat". Cliffhanger slide.
5. Proof two. The film: their photos on the screen.
6. Ana: "hi" / "ive been here the whole time" / "ive watched it 4 times" / "best surprise ive ever ruined" ❤️. Then Tom: "ok but link. i need one for my mum".
7. The save. "for tom and the 300 people in my comments": template, ticket → cake → film, "we split it between the 4 of us", code LOVE, "save this for the group chat".
