# Featured dates: who paid

A featured date is a place a venue pays to have marked "Featured" (Spanish "Destacado"), in words,
wherever the Dates pages list it. The label is all it buys: the place keeps its rank, every filter
still applies to it, and reviews and votes stay the couples' own. The page for venues is `/dates/for-venues`.
