## Why this one works where 85's voice did not

85 was written like a novel — "Good. Sit down.", "The end, Mara." It has the right psychology and the
wrong mouth. Nobody types like that, and this audience can spot a fake screenshot in half a second.
The same obsession works far harder when it comes out of a boy who is embarrassed about it:

- **"ok dont be weird about this"** is what a real 21-year-old says before a confession, and it does
  the same job as "Sit down" without costing the post its realism.
- **The evidence is third-party.** He screenshotted her first message to his group chat at 1am and
  wrote "im in trouble". A witness who is not in love with her says he was gone from the start, which
  is worth more than anything he could say himself.
- **"and then i took 2 days"** is the detail that makes it true. He panicked first and played it cool
  second, which is exactly what people do and nobody ever admits.
- **"i rewrote it like 9 times" / "nine"** — a number does the confessing, and she only repeats it.
- **"its mine"** is the whole post. Flat delivery of an enormous feeling, two words, no punctuation.
  That is what gets screenshotted, and it is the realistic cousin of 85's "Youre not running now."
- **He counts from the day she messaged him, not from the first date.** Same machine as 78, said like
  a person instead of a narrator.

Her: Ellie. Him: the one with the receipts. Nobody is a bad anything, so the comments go to "im asking
my bf what he sent his mates", which is the comment that turns into a sale.
