## Why this one

A riddle the viewer plays along with: the password is the first thing she ever said to him, and she has to guess it. It is a real feature (a gift can be locked; the editor's own placeholder is "Something only they'd know"). The caption turns the riddle on the viewer, which is the comment engine from our own hit, 30: a question everyone can answer about their own life. Maker in blue, so the feeling left is "I could do that".
