## Why this one

Every post so far is two silly crimes and a happy mess. This is the other engine, the one that gets more comments than anything on the app: **he did something wrong and used the gift to get out of it.** Every line of his is a tactic people will name in the comments, which is the point:

- "ur going through my likes now?": makes her the problem.
- "i was up til 3 making u this and THIS is what u lead with": the gift as a debt she owes.
- "i love u so much maya. i love spoiling u": the love-bomb, with the gift as its proof.
- "ur being so ungrateful rn" / "other girls would be in tears": ungrateful, and replaceable.
- "say it back": the demand.
- Her "idk", and under it **Read 00:12**. He read it and went quiet. The last word on the screen is a read receipt.

She never says what she thinks. The caption asks the viewer instead, and ten thousand people will tell her. The camps are "run" and "am i overreacting" (his words, which is why she is asking), with a third arguing that the gift was real and the like was nothing.

## What this does to the brand, and how it is handled

In this story the gift is the manipulator's tool, and the link card says tryethos.io. Left alone, a share of the comments becomes "so this is what toxic boyfriends use". Three things keep it on the right side:

1. **Maya is the narrator, and she is not sold.** The post is her doubting it. The account is on the viewer's side.
2. **The pinned comment does not cheer, and it does not carry the code.** It answers "what app" honestly and stays in character (below). The code goes in a reply, in a line that turns the story round: "use it on someone who deserves it".
3. **It is one post.** The happy ones on either side of it are the account's voice; this is the account showing it can tell the other story too. Do not run two of these in a row.

This will get the most comments of the batch and probably the fewest sales: "views are not sales". It is a reach post. It brings people to the account, where the next post sells.
