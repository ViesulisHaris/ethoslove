# Would-you-rather questions: shortlist (NOT approved)

Written and scored by six role-played women aged 19 to 24 (a 1.5-year relationship, a situationship veteran, long distance, just out of a toxic relationship, three months in, the group-chat organiser). Scores are 1 to 5 for "would I play this with my partner and send the screenshot to the group chat". The same model wrote and scored them, so treat the scores as a filter for flat ideas, not as real audience data. Only posting them tells us which work.

None of these is approved. The owner picks.

## Crazy (starts a real fight)

1. Their ex becomes your best friend / Your ex becomes their best friend
2. Get dumped for your best friend / Get dumped for their ex
3. Learn he almost broke up with you last month / Learn he nearly kissed someone else last month
4. Keep your partner, lose your best friend / Keep your best friend, lose your partner
5. He told his friends your deepest secret / He told your ex your deepest secret

## Medium (tense and relatable)

1. Your best friend hates your partner / Their best friend hates you
2. Group chat reads your fight texts / They read what you vented to friends
3. They check your phone every night / They delete their own chats every night
4. See their dating app still active / Find a folder of their ex's photos

## Mild (a light argument)

1. Partner posts your worst photo on their story / Partner never posts you, ever
2. Only voice notes / Only one-word texts

## What the audience said

- Strongest: triangles with an ex or a best friend, and what the partner said or did behind your back.
- Flat, every time: money, birthdays, matching outfits, late vs early, shared food. If one option is obviously better, nobody argues.
- "Keep your best friend, lose your partner" fits the "how could you?!" opening best, since his answer is the shock.
