## How to post a part 2

- **Answer the comments in the first slide's hook: "you asked what was in it".** No recap. The people
  watching either saw part 1 or they will go and find it, which is a second view of the first post.
- **Pin the link to part 1** in the comments, and pin "part 2 is up" on part 1. The two posts feed each
  other for about 48 hours.
- **Every gift frame belongs here.** Part 1 showed nothing, so this is where the six frames of the
  scrapbook land on an audience that has been waiting a day to see them. That is the best-primed
  product shot we will ever get.
- **Keep the payoff small.** Do not show the whole letter. One page, one line, one number: he started
  it in November, they got together in January, and he waited seven months to say he fancied her.

you are shooting. Two things have to be true in the frames: there is a page about a bus stop, and the
last page is readable in one still.
