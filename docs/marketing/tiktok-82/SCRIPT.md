## Why this one is built to sell

- **It sells the extra, not the template.** From the sales data, the money on a gift is the song, the
  voice note and the extra photos. "is that YOUR VOICE" in capitals is worth more than any description
  of the pages, because nobody knows you can put your own voice in one until they see somebody find it.
- **"Dont put it on speaker" is a hook and a warning label.** The viewer knows something is coming
  four slides before it does, and the payoff is the funniest possible version of it: her family heard
  it at breakfast.
- **Nobody is embarrassed in a bad way.** He is shy, she is delighted, the family is an accident. The
  comments will be "I would DIE" and "what did he say", not an argument about either of them.
- **The gift is a thing with pages,** which is what makes somebody who cannot write a letter think
  they could still do this: photos, tape, handwriting, and one twenty-second recording.
- **It is a birthday in the morning,** which is when people search for last-minute presents. Post it
  the night before the buying peak and it is in the feed when they need it.
