## Why this one

Dread → relief, the oldest lever in the doc, on the widest possible recipient. "It's fine love. I know you're busy." is a line every child has received; the hook opens the wound, the gift closes it at 12:00 exactly. Mum's full sentences and "Your father is missing this" are the comedy that carries the tenderness.

## The slides

1. The stop. Her grey bubble first — "It's fine love. I know you're busy." — then blue scrambling: "mum" / "im not busy" / "look at ur phone at 12" / "I am looking at my phone."
2. The turn. 12:00 AM, the link card "50 balloons for mum", "Do I pop them", "There is a picture in this one." / "Oh" / "Oh that's the day we got Nube".

6. "I've read it three times" / "I didn't think you remembered the bike." / "i remember all of it" / "I'm going to wake your father." ❤️ / "Can you make one for Auntie Sue".
