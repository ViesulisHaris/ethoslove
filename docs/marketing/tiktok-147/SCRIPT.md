## Why this shape

It is the shape of a carousel that did 2.9M views for another gift site: a cosy, nothing-is-happening opener (are u alive / barely. i have 3 deadlines / u have had 3 deadlines since 2023), four slides of banter before the link appears, and the link only on slide 5. There is no hook over the first slide; the banter is the hook, and five bubbles on a black page read in two seconds, so people swipe all ten. What this one adds:

- **A loop.** "3 deadlines" and "4 minutes" both come back on the last slide, and the last bubble is "told u" for the second time. People go back to slide 1 to check, and reverse swipes count.
- **Two keyboards.** One of them types in lowercase, the other's phone capitalises for them. You can tell who is talking without looking at the colours, which is what a real screenshot looks like.
- **A reaction that stays in character.** Nobody says "this is the most beautiful thing". She says she is on the floor with wet hair.
