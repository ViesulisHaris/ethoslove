# TikTok carousel 77 · "be normal about it" (the slow one, 10 slides)

Post any week, from the couple account, 22:00 UK. This one is built on the 2.9M post's shape, not ours.

## What the 2.9M post does that our first 76 do not

A creator's carousel found under the search **"free website for love letter"** did 2.9M views, 515K
likes, 56.4K saves and 16.1K shares with a story where *nothing happens*: "You in bed yet?" / "almost"
/ "You always say almost" / "because i'm still doing my skincare" / "Right right". Two full slides of
that before he says "Btw · I made you something earlier". The link does not appear until slide 5 of 10.

Three things it does that we were not doing:

1. **It is slow on purpose.** Ten slides, five of them chat, and the first two have no stakes at all.
   Our carousels front-load the hook in bubble one; this one earns the swipe with familiarity instead.
   Ten slides is ten swipes, and swipes are the whole ranking signal on a photo post.
2. **The bubbles are tiny.** Two to five words: "Rude", "Btw", "Fair", "skill issue". Ours run twelve
   to fifteen. Short bubbles are read, long ones are skimmed.
3. **The gift arrives as an afterthought, not a gesture.** "I made you something earlier" is thrown
   away mid-conversation, and *he* is the one playing it down ("It's not that serious", "Maybe a
   little" cheesy). Nobody is crying, nobody is in a car park at 4am. It is cosy, not dramatic, and
   cosy is what gets saved — 56.4K saves is people keeping it as an idea, not a story.

What it is missing, and what this one adds:

- **A running joke.** Theirs opens on skincare and never returns to it. Ours is "you have to be normal
  about it", said three times, and the last slide is "i wasnt normal about it". Comments will quote
  that line, which is the cheapest comment engine there is.
- **A number.** "Since Sunday" / "its thursday" does the confessing without anyone saying a feeling out
  loud, and it is a four-day version of effort any 20-year-old can actually copy.
- **A loop.** Her first word is "no" and her last word is "no". People swipe back to check, and a
  second pass through ten slides is a second watch.
- **One withheld thing.** "Don't skip to the end" plants a P.S. nobody has read yet, so slide 10's "the
  ps ruined me" sends them to the link to find out what it says.

## Why this one should beat it

Theirs is ten slides of charm with nothing to argue about; the comments are compliments, which are the
cheapest kind. This one hands over a phrase to repeat ("be normal about it"), a number to check
("since Sunday" against "its thursday"), a thing nobody has read (the P.S.) and a loop that pays a
second swipe. Same cosy, same slow, four more reasons to type something.
