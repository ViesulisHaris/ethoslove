# Journalist requests (HARO and Source of Sources)

Started 9 Nov 2026. Writers of gift guides and relationship pieces post requests for people to quote;
a quote that gets used usually comes with a link to tryethos.io from a real news or lifestyle site,
which is the kind of link that moves Google most (`LINK-KIT.md`, `SEO-ORGANIC.md`).

## How it runs

Set up 9 Nov 2026:

1. **HARO**: support@secuora.xyz is subscribed and verified (helpareporter.com, free). Emails at
   5:35, 12:35 and 17:35 New York time (12:35, 19:35 and 00:35 in Riga). Source of Sources is gone
   (sosrcs.com no longer exists).
2. **Featured.com** (free plan, the company behind HARO): account on support@secuora.xyz, and an
   "All-in-One" workflow limited to journalist requests and HARO, matched to a description of Ethos,
   emailed to support@secuora.xyz every day at 8:00 Riga. Featured never contacts journalists itself;
   its requests are answered on featured.com.
3. **The owner's answers** are below, written once in their own words (typos fixed, nothing reworded).
4. **Each day at 13:00 Riga** a scheduled task on the owner's PC (Claude app, "HARO: daily reply
   drafts", taskId haro-daily-drafts; runs while the app is open) reads only the HARO and Featured
   emails in Spacemail, picks up to three requests the answers fit, and leaves replies in Spacemail
   Drafts. Featured items are listed for the owner to answer on featured.com. It never sends.
5. **The owner reads the drafts and sends them.**

Why the owner's own words: HARO's owner runs every pitch through an AI-text detector. In its own
figures about 21% of 1.39 million pitches were flagged as fully AI-written, about a third of
journalists chose to block those, and human-written pitches were picked about three times as often.

Rules for every answer: only true facts (prices, templates and features as the site has them),
no sales talk, answer the question in the first sentence, sign off once:
"[Name], founder of Ethos (tryethos.io)".

## The answer bank

The questions gift and relationship writers ask most. Answer each in your own words.

1. What's a meaningful gift for a partner in a long-distance relationship?
2. What's a good last-minute gift that doesn't feel last-minute (birthday, Christmas, Valentine's)?
3. Are digital gifts as meaningful as physical ones? Why are more people sending them?
4. What's a thoughtful gift under $20 for a partner?
5. What do you get someone who says they want nothing, or has everything?
6. How do you make any gift feel personal?
7. What are people sending for Valentine's Day this year? What's changed?
8. First anniversary (or one-year dating) gift ideas?
9. How do you write a love letter or a card message when you're not a writer?
10. What's a good gift a few months into a new relationship, without overdoing it?
11. Gift ideas for a mom, a best friend or a sibling, beyond a card?
12. Why did you start Ethos? What did you see that was missing?
13. What's the biggest lesson from running a small online business? How did you find your first
    customers?
14. Are digital gifts a greener choice (no shipping, no wrapping, nothing thrown away)?
15. What's the most common mistake people make when choosing a gift?

## Free codes for writers

A pitch can carry a free "Give Everything" code (every gift, forever, single use) so the writer can
make a gift themself. It is a gift card with no purchase behind it, added in Supabase with the
owner's OK each time, and redeemed at tryethos.io/redeem (this works while gift cards aren't on
sale). Codes go only in the email, never in this file: the mirror of this repository is public.
