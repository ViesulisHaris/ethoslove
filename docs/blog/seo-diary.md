# SEO decision diary

One entry a day from the daily SEO routine (`docs/blog/SEO-AGENT.md`), newest first: what changed,
the data behind it, what it cost, and when to check whether it worked.

## 2026-11-13: monthiversary-ideas aimed at "monthiversary"
- **Page:** /blog/monthiversary-ideas
- **Why:** Search Console has it at 8.2 for "monthiversary" (33 impressions a week, no clicks).
  DataForSEO, US: "monthiversary" 1,300 a month, difficulty 0, informational; "monthsary" 1,000
  (KD 7); "happy monthiversary" 590 (KD 0); "1 month anniversary ideas" 260 (KD 0); the post's own
  keyword, "monthiversary ideas", only 10. The top 10 are Q&A threads, Reddit and an etymology
  column about what the word means and whether a month counts; the AI overview defines it, names
  monthsary and mensiversary and cites Reddit, Wiktionary and two YouTube Shorts (a place Ethos could
  appear). People also ask: how do you say one month anniversary, what is a 1 month celebration
  called, how often should couples go on dates, what do you call a 3 year anniversary. The post only
  promised ideas.
- **What changed:** Title "Monthiversary: what it means, 12 ideas and what to say", a lead and
  section that define it and its other names (monthsary, mensiversary, why purists object), a
  "1 month anniversary ideas" section, happy monthiversary lines, every PAA question answered
  (two new FAQs). Row 12 became a real plan. Spanish to match. `updated` 2026-11-13.
- **Panel:** 10/10/10 after 2 rounds (`docs/blog/reviews/refresh/monthiversary-ideas-2026-11-13.json`)
- **Cost:** $0.03 (weekly ranks $0.012, SERP $0.002, keyword overview $0.013); balance $4.54
- **Check on:** 2026-11-30 whether "monthiversary" moved from 8.2 onto the top five and got clicks,
  and whether "monthsary" and "happy monthiversary" show up for the post
