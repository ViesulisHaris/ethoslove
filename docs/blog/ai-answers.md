# What AI answers say

Every Thursday the daily SEO routine (`docs/blog/SEO-AGENT.md`, step 1) asks ChatGPT, with web
search as a US user, the ten buyer questions from `docs/marketing/KEYWORDS.md` ("AI answers") and
records here whether Ethos is named, which brands are, and which pages are cited. Newest section
first. The answers that matter are buyers' questions; a page they cite that leaves Ethos out is a
link prospect (`docs/blog/link-prospects.md`).

## 2026-11-13 (the research batch, the baseline)

Ethos was named in 1 of 10 answers: 4th of 4 for "website to make a love letter with photos and
music", with a correct summary of The Letter. The other nine named makers such as 2Luv, Letters by
Heart, LovePanda, Digi Bouquet, Canva and GiftFeels, and cited comparison pages written by makers
(ouvella.com in 4 of 10, stampgoose.com, RAYXIS's own page) and long-distance gift guides. Two of
our posts were already cited by ChatGPT elsewhere: /blog/cute-things-to-say-to-your-girlfriend and
/blog/paragraphs-for-your-girlfriend. The full table is in KEYWORDS.md.
